Biography

I am an incoming undergraduate student at Carnegie Mellon University, and an interdisciplinary musician and researcher. My main areas include music AI, human-machine interaction, and computer-based sound design. Broadly speaking, I am interested in the use of computer technology in augmenting traditional music, exploring new performance possibilities and preserving culture.

I’m currently focused on the music of Greece. I am the founder of the Athens Music Technology Forum (AMTF), a non-profit organisation with the aim of promoting research and education in computer science, music, and the Greek culture. Some of my work at AMTF include Choros, an interactive map for Greek dances, as well as the Audio Lab, a collection of web tools for artists.

As a musician, I play piano, guitar, and the Chinese erhu (which I recently started learning). I have composed over 30 compositions, some of which you can listen to here. I have also performed an extensive musical repertoire ranging from classical music to rock, and more recently jazz. As a member of my school orchestra, I have served as the keyboardist, melodica player, and bassist.

Some of my side gigs include making electronic music, live looping & coding, street busking, designing cool digital instruments, and ice hockey. I also run an eponymous YouTube channel, where I frequently upload my performances, compositions and covers.
